TripSync Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 (updated for TripSync 1.1)
App: TripSync by DreamKey Automation

TripSync helps you plan a group trip, log shared expenses and see who owes whom. This page explains what happens to your information. The short version: we collect nothing.

No account, no data collected

TripSync has no sign-up and no login. DreamKey Automation does not collect, receive, store or share any personal information or usage data from the app.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter, including trips, people, expenses, budgets and settlements, is saved only in local storage on your own iPhone. TripSync has no server and no cloud sync, and it works fully offline.

No analytics, no tracking, no third parties

TripSync contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code or services. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

UPI payments

If you tap “Pay via UPI”, TripSync opens the UPI app already on your phone with the payee and amount filled in. The payment is made entirely inside your own UPI app. TripSync never sees, receives or stores your payment details, bank information or payment results. The UPI ID you type for a person is saved only on your device.

Sharing a trip summary

If you tap Share, TripSync hands a plain-text summary to the iOS share sheet. What happens next depends on the app you choose, such as WhatsApp, and is governed by that app’s own privacy policy. TripSync does not send the summary anywhere itself.

Trip Fund and reminders

The Trip Fund is a record you keep inside TripSync of what each person in your group says they have sent to the person collecting for the trip. TripSync does not hold, move or handle any money, and it does not confirm payments. If you turn on the optional weekly reminder, TripSync asks your permission and then schedules a notification on your own iPhone. The reminder is created and shown on your device only; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. The “Request share” and “Nudge” buttons only hand a text message to the iOS share sheet, and what happens next depends on the app you choose.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete all data in TripSync to erase every trip, person, expense and settlement from your device. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone.

Children

TripSync does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
